## What the footage establishes

- Independent public-browser session: Lightning v1 5/8, Lightning v2 12/16, then Super with prompt v2 and a different review-all policy v2 at 16/16. That last result is a supervised draft workflow, not a model-only ranking or autonomous-triage success.
- The public session made 57 actual provider calls. Its held-out draft was stored for human review, its live probe compared 16 new observations with 16 recorded baseline observations, and its stale approval refused the next board action without undoing the completed draft.
- The later fixture segment is a separate controlled synthetic experiment: 80 observations, injected signal, hold, blocked future action, and fallback requiring fresh qualification and approval. It does not claim a real provider drift incident.
- Every failed live qualification remains in the exported evidence. Low-power probes and known-answer synthetic cases do not establish general safety, correctness, equivalence or causation.
